About Student Reminder App

Helping Students Stay Organized, On Track, and Ahead

Student Reminder App is a free, student-focused web application built to help you manage assignments, exams, lectures, and reminders — all in one clean, simple place.

PROJECT INTRODUCTION

What is Student Reminder App?

Student Reminder App is an academic organization tool that gives students a single, intuitive place to track the things that matter most — assignments, examinations, lectures, practicals, and personal reminders.

Juggling multiple subjects, deadlines, and schedules can quickly become overwhelming. We built Student Reminder App to replace scattered sticky notes and spreadsheets with one clean, reliable system designed specifically for how students actually study and work.

Never miss a deadline again

Less stress, more confidence

Always know what's due and when — before it becomes urgent.
