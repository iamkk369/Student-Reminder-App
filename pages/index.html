FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

Is Student Reminder App really free?

Yes! The app is completely free for students, forever. No credit card required, no hidden fees.

Can I sync with my existing calendar?

Yes. You can sync with Google Calendar, Apple Calendar, and other popular calendar apps.

Does it work on mobile?

Absolutely. The app is fully responsive and works seamlessly on any device — phone, tablet, or desktop.

How do I get reminded about deadlines?

You'll receive email reminders and in-app notifications before your deadlines. You can customize the timing.

Is my data private?

Yes. Your academic data belongs to you. We never share, sell, or monetize your personal information.

Which devices are supported?

The web app works in all modern browsers. Mobile-ready design ensures a great experience everywhere.
